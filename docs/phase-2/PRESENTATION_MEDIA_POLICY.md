# Presentation Media Policy

The Three.js town scene, generated GLB, neutral CSS fallback, and
two-dimensional relationship diagram are presentation media.

They may:

- visualize relationships supported by approved manuscript evidence;
- use non-semantic connective geometry only to visualize a source-supported
  spatial relationship;
- use inferred positions when clearly classified internally and visibly;
- provide accessible static fallbacks and reduced-motion behavior.

They may not:

- claim surveyed or canonical coordinates;
- create independent public place records;
- establish an event, relationship, address, or chronology;
- turn filler geometry into source evidence;
- hide uncertainty.

Generated scenic fallback artwork is not permitted. If the GLB cannot load,
the interface must use a neutral surface that introduces no landmarks,
structures, incidents, people, or places.

Named selectable nodes must retain stable provenance-aware naming. Source
relative and inferred placements remain visually distinguishable. The map and
scene share the revision-pinned spatial plan so they cannot drift silently.
